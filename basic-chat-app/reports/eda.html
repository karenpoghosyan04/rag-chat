What this means

The data are small and mostly answerable. Pair labels are strongly imbalanced: most passages do not match the supplied evidence. An unmatched passage is only a weak negative; it can still contain useful information. Use average precision and recall instead of reporting majority-class accuracy.

Normalize text for matching, preserve page/section metadata, and split questions before creating pairs. StandardScaler is fitted inside each training fold for the supervised models. The live embedding normalization, chunking, thresholds, and reranker remain unchanged.

Correlations describe associations, not causes. Feature analysis excludes the nine previously held-out questions. Same-document question splitting does not measure generalization to new documents. Gather new PDFs with manually reviewed labels and split by document for a final test.
